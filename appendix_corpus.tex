\section*{Appendix A. The 100 included studies}\label{app:corpus}

Table~\ref{tab:corpus} lists every record in the retained corpus, so that the
included-study set is visible in the paper itself and not only in the released
data. IDs are corpus record numbers, cited in the running text as R$<$ID$>$ to keep
them distinct from bracketed reference numbers [n]; the ID also doubles as the
bibliography key (\texttt{ref$<$ID$>$}), and the \emph{Ref.}\ column of
Table~\ref{tab:substantive} gives the mapping for the substantive studies. The IDs
follow the retained rank order of the selection rule: year descending, and within
each year block citations descending (IDs 1--45 are all 2026), so the ID order is
effectively a recency ordering---a property of the realised ranking, not of the
literature (Section~\ref{sec:method}). ``FT'' marks the 83 records coded at full
text; the 17 abstract-only records were coded at abstract level, with full-text
fields treated as missing. R18, R59, R69 and R97 fall outside the strict grid scope of
Section~\ref{sec:ai-threats}; R34, R68 and R84 are the borderline cases there.
