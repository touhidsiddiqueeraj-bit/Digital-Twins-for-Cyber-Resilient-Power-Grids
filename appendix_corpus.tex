\section*{Appendix A. The 100 included studies}\label{app:corpus}

Table~\ref{tab:corpus} lists every record in the retained corpus, so that the
included-study set is visible in the paper itself and not only in the released
data. Citation scheme: IDs are corpus record numbers, cited in the running text as
R$<$ID$>$; each record's full bibliographic entry appears in the reference list under
the key \texttt{ref$<$ID$>$} (with DOI where one exists), so no separate mapping
between the two schemes is needed. The IDs
are a display order---year descending, and within each year block citations
descending (IDs 1--45 are all 2026)---as the Cit.\ column shows. That ordering is
a property of the export, not evidence about the selection rule that retained
these 100 of the 492 eligible records; the comparison that would establish the
rule is the year distribution of the 492 eligible records against the retained
100, and the candidate-pool export it requires is listed as an unreleased
artifact in the search-provenance manifest (Section~\ref{sec:method}).
``FT'' marks the 83 records coded at full text; the 17 abstract-only records
were coded at abstract level, with full-text fields treated as missing.
R18, R59, R69 and R97 fall outside the strict grid scope of
Section~\ref{sec:ai-threats}; R34, R68 and R84 are the borderline cases there.
R69 carries unattributed authorship (Section~\ref{sec:validity}).
