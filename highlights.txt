Highlights
- 100-study PRISMA corpus on digital twins for power-grid cybersecurity, 2020-2026.
- AI is defensive in 70 of 83 studies; 35 of 83 (32-53%) engage any AI-enabled threat, 11 substantively (fragile).
- Only one study treats the digital twin itself as an explicit attack target.
- Coverage sits at 66 of 83 at operation, one at design, none at commissioning (as coded).
- Five-item agenda: each names its gap and the evidence that would close it.
